Stubb tells Flask about a dream: Ahab kicked him with the ivory leg, and when Stubb raged at the insult, a humpbacked merman appeared and told him to regard it as an honour — a kick from a great man is worth more than a punch from a lesser one. Stubb woke, half-convinced. Meanwhile Ahab has already ordered extra lookouts aloft, calling for them to watch specifically for a white whale. The hunt has quietly begun.
