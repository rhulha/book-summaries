Father Mapple delivers a thunderous sermon on Jonah: his disobedience to God, his guilty flight aboard a ship to Tarshish, the storm, the casting of lots, the whale. He draws two lessons — for sinners, true repentance is not demanding pardon but accepting punishment; for preachers, the duty is to speak truth even to a hostile world. The sermon ends with an ecstatic declaration that the greatest delight belongs to the man who acknowledges no lord but God.
