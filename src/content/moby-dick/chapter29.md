Warm tropical weather draws Ahab to pace the deck through the night, his ivory leg hammering a relentless rhythm that disturbs the sleeping crew. Stubb politely approaches and suggests he muffle the leg. Ahab erupts — calls him a dog and orders him below. Stubb retreats, stunned and baffled, and delivers a long soliloquy in his hammock trying to make sense of the encounter. He decides Ahab must be "a sort of a strange critter, and not a common man."
