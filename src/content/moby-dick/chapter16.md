Queequeg's god Yojo has ordained that Ishmael alone must choose their ship. Ishmael tours the harbour and selects the Pequod: an ancient, weatherbeaten vessel decorated with sperm-whale teeth along her bulwarks and a tiller carved from a whale's jaw — "a cannibal of a craft." He meets the gruff Quaker co-owner Captain Peleg and the miserly Captain Bildad. After a comic haggling scene — Bildad proposing the 777th lay; Peleg insisting on the 300th — Ishmael signs on. He learns that the real captain, Ahab, lost his leg to a whale and is brooding ashore, not yet seen by anyone.
