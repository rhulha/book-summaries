A comic account of mealtimes aboard the Pequod. Ahab dines first, in heavy silence, and the three mates sit in reverential tension, barely daring to speak. Starbuck ventures a remark; Stubb tries a joke that lands badly; Flask eats in frantic haste, always last in and first out, perpetually half-starved. After the mates withdraw, the three harpooneers take their places at the table and eat with magnificent, terrifying appetite, reducing the timid steward Dough-Boy to a state of permanent nervous collapse.
