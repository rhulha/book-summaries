Ishmael brings Queequeg to sign on to the Pequod. Peleg and Bildad refuse to accept a cannibal. Ishmael talks his way around their objections by invoking the "great and everlasting First Congregation of this whole worshipping world." Queequeg clinches the argument by harpooning a small tar spot at great distance, skimming the iron over Bildad's hat. He is signed on for the 90th lay. Bildad presses a religious tract into his hands; Peleg warns that "pious harpooneers never make good voyagers."
