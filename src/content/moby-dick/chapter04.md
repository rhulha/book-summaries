Ishmael wakes to find Queequeg's tattooed arm thrown over him. The patchwork quilt and the arm blend together in colour. He recalls a childhood memory of waking in the dark to feel a supernatural hand holding his — an experience that paralyzed him with nameless dread. He watches Queequeg's comic morning toilet: boots put on under the bed, face shaved with a harpoon blade. Queequeg departs proudly, harpoon carried like a marshal's baton.
