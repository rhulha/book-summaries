The pivotal scene of the novel. Ahab summons all hands to the quarter-deck and nails a sixteen-dollar Spanish gold doubloon to the main-mast: it belongs to whoever first sights Moby Dick, the White Whale. He confirms that it was Moby Dick who reaped off his leg. Starbuck challenges him — to seek vengeance on a dumb animal is madness; it is blasphemy to hate a beast. Ahab responds with his famous "pasteboard masks" speech: all visible things are but walls and illusions; the white whale is the mask through which he strikes at the inscrutable force behind the world. The harpooneers cross their weapons and drink from the iron sockets of their harpoons in a dark pagan oath. The entire crew is swept up in Ahab's frenzy.
