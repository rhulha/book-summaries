In the grey pre-dawn, Ishmael and Queequeg head to the Pequod and pass Elijah one last time. He mutters darkly about shadows he thought he saw boarding the ship in the night. They find the vessel hushed and apparently empty, then discover a rigger who confirms that Ahab came aboard late the previous night. Starbuck is already active on deck. The mystery of Ahab deepens as the ship readies to sail.
