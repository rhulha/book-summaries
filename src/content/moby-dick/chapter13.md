Ishmael and Queequeg take the schooner Moss to Nantucket. Queequeg exchanges wheelbarrow stories with Ishmael (each culture baffled by the other's customs). On board, a country bumpkin mocks Queequeg; Queequeg hurls him into the air. Minutes later, when a snapped boom sends the same man overboard, Queequeg dives in and rescues him without fanfare — "We cannibals must help these Christians." All hands vote him a noble trump.
