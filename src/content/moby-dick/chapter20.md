The Pequod is being furiously provisioned for departure: sails mended, rigging overhauled, the hold stocked. Bildad's warm-hearted sister Aunt Charity bustles aboard daily with jars of pickles, rolls of flannel, and eventually an oil-ladle and a whaling-lance. Captain Ahab remains ashore, still unseen. Ishmael tries to suppress his growing unease about sailing under a captain he has never laid eyes on.
