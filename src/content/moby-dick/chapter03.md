Ishmael explores the inn: a mysterious smoke-blackened painting of a whale impaling itself on a sinking ship's masts, walls hung with harpoons and cannibals' clubs. The inn is full — he must share a bed with an unknown harpooneer. He waits anxiously. When the harpooneer finally arrives, he is Queequeg: heavily tattooed, purple-complexioned, carrying an embalmed head and a tomahawk-pipe, performing idol-worship by candlelight. After initial terror, Ishmael reasons that "a sober cannibal is better than a drunken Christian" and they sleep soundly together.
