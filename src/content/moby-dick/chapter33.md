An essay on shipboard authority and ceremony. In the old Dutch whale-fishery, the "Specksynder" — the Chief Harpooneer — was the true commander; the captain was a later, landlubber imposition. Ishmael reflects on how Ahab maintains his authority through ceremony and mystique, keeping himself partly absent and remote so that his presence, when felt, strikes with full force. True greatness, he concludes, cannot be explained by office or rank alone — it must be "plucked from the skies."
