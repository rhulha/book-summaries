Leaving the Pequod, Ishmael and Queequeg are stopped by a ragged, pockmarked stranger who calls himself Elijah. He makes dark, cryptic allusions to Captain Ahab — a Cape Horn episode, a "scrimshage with a Spaniard before the altar," a silver calabash, a prophecy about the leg. Ishmael dismisses him as a humbug. But Elijah follows them at a distance, and afterward the encounter lingers uneasily in Ishmael's mind.
