The first of two chapters introducing the Pequod's officers. Starbuck, the chief mate, is a lean, earnest Nantucket Quaker — careful, conscientious, and brave, but with a bravery rooted in deep awareness of danger rather than its absence. His courage is not the reckless kind: "I will have no man in my boat who is not afraid of a whale." Ishmael pauses to meditate on the dignity of common men, arguing that democratic ideals demand we honour the meanest mariners and renegades as fully human.
