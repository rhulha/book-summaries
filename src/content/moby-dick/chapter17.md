Queequeg spends the day in religious fasting, seated motionless on his haunches in their locked room with his idol Yojo balanced on his head. Ishmael, unable to rouse him, panics and nearly breaks down the door — Mrs. Hussey fears another suicide. Queequeg is found perfectly serene. When the Ramadan ends, Ishmael lectures him at length on the irrationality of extreme religious practice. Queequeg listens politely and is entirely unimpressed.
