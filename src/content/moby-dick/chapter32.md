Ishmael interrupts the narrative for an extended scientific classification of whales. He divides the order into three "Books" by size: the Folio (Sperm Whale, Right Whale, Fin-Back, Hump-Back, Razor-Back, Sulphur-Bottom), the Octavo (Grampus, Black Fish, Narwhale, Killer, Thrasher), and the Duodecimo (three species of porpoise). Each species receives a brief description covering its habitat, physical traits, and usefulness to the fishery. Ishmael acknowledges his system is imperfect and leaves it deliberately unfinished, "like the great Cathedral of Cologne" — a structure whose incompleteness is itself a testament to human ambition.
