A theatrical ensemble scene in near-dramatic form, with speech headings for sailors of different nationalities. In the wake of Ahab's quarter-deck ceremony, the crew celebrates below decks — singing, dancing, Pip beating his tambourine. A squall line approaches. Tensions flare: Daggoo and a Spanish sailor nearly come to blows over a racial insult. The wind rises to a shriek. In the tumult, Pip, small and terrified, crouches and prays aloud to God to protect "this one poor fin-back whale" of a Black boy from those who have "no bowels to feel fear in." The chapter ends in darkness and storm.
