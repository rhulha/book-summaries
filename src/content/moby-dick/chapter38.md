Starbuck's soliloquy at the mainmast in the gathering dark. He sees with terrible clarity that Ahab's madness will destroy them all, and he cannot stop it — his duty of obedience and Ahab's force of will overmaster his reason. "I think I see his impious end; but feel that I must help him to it." He prays that some power from outside might intervene where he cannot. His helplessness and his grief are rendered with quiet dignity.
