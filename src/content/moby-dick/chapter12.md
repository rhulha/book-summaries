Queequeg is a prince from the remote island of Rokovoko, son of a High Chief. Burning to see Christendom, he secretly stowed away on a Sag Harbour whaling ship by paddling out in a canoe and clinging to the anchor chains. Onboard, he learned whalemen's skills. But witnessing Christian behaviour in port — the drinking, the waste, the cruelty — disillusioned him completely: "it's a wicked world in all meridians; I'll die a pagan." He now agrees to ship with Ishmael to Nantucket.
