Ahab appears on deck for the first time. He is a tall, broad man of bronzed, weathered aspect — neither old nor young, but aged beyond years. A livid white scar runs from his hairline down the full length of his body, as if he had been struck by lightning. One leg is ivory, carved from a sperm-whale's jaw, its base planted in an auger-hole drilled into the deck. He stands at the rail staring fixedly ahead with an expression of unbending, unsurrenderable will. He says nothing. As the weather warms on the voyage south, he begins to appear on deck more regularly.
