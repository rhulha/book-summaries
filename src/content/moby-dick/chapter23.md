A short, elegiac chapter. Bulkington, the powerful sailor briefly glimpsed in New Bedford, is seen at the helm as the ship heads out into the gale. Ishmael meditates on his meaning: the soul that seeks safety in harbour will be dashed on the lee shore; only by driving into the open, howling sea can it survive. "In landlessness alone resides the highest truth." Bulkington is given a six-inch chapter as his "stoneless grave."
