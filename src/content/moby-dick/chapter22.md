The Pequod departs on Christmas Day. Captain Peleg roars commands and literally kicks reluctant hands into action; Captain Bildad paces the deck singing psalms. As the ship clears the harbour and the pilots prepare to disembark, Bildad's farewell becomes unexpectedly tender — he calls out last reminders about flannel waistcoats and not forgetting to say prayers. The pilots descend into their boat and the Pequod plunges out into the wintry Atlantic alone.
